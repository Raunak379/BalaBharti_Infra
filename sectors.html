Sectors

Geosynthetics solve the same core problems — weak ground, steep slopes, erosion, settlement — across every kind of infrastructure. Here is where our systems are most often used.

Roads & Highways

Reinforced soil walls for widening and grade separation, biaxial geogrid in the pavement, geocell slope protection and geotextile separation over weak subgrade.

Railways & Metro

Basal reinforcement and formation stabilisation, reinforced soil approaches and cutting slope protection along corridors and depots.

Bridges & Structures

Reinforced soil abutments and approach ramps, GFRP reinforcement for decks and approach slabs, and drainage geocomposites behind structures.

Hill Roads & Landslide Zones

Rockfall drape and anchored mesh, soil nailing, reinforced soil slopes for valley-side widening and erosion control on cut faces.

Water Resources & Irrigation

Geocell and gabion channel linings, canal slope protection, reservoir embankment reinforcement and filtration layers under rip-rap.

Ports & Coastal

Reinforced fills for yards and stacking areas, GFRP reinforcement in the splash zone, geotextile filters and scour protection.

Mining & Ash Dykes

Basal reinforcement for dykes and heaps, steepened reinforced slopes to save footprint, and erosion control on raised embankments.

Industrial & Real Estate

Retaining structures for plot-level differences, ground improvement under warehouses and hardstands, and site erosion control.
